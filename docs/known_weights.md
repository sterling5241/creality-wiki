# Known Weights

Items that add up to about 6 kg (13 lb), for [Load Cell calibration](loadcells.md#calibrate-the-load-cells).

Weigh whatever you use on a kitchen scale and enter the real weight, packaging and brands vary.

## Packaged goods

The weight is printed on the package, the packaging adds a little extra.

| Items | Approx total | lb |
|-------|--------------|----|
| 3 x 2 kg bag of sugar, flour or rice | 6000 g | 13.2 |
| 6 x 1 kg bag of sugar, flour or rice | 6000 g | 13.2 |
| 12 x 500 g pasta or coffee | 6000 g | 13.2 |
